Connect your Twilio account to run Ringg agents on your Twilio numbers; they then call and answer like any other [number](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/overview). This is one of the [bring-your-own-telephony](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/your-own-telephony/byot-overview) options. Twilio appears in **Add number** only when international calling is enabled for your workspace, because Ringg lists Twilio numbers for the United States and Canada. There are no Ringg-provided Twilio numbers.

## Connect the account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Twilio account form">
    Go to **Numbers → Add number**, select **Twilio**, and open **Configure provider** (the gear). Select **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields and save">
    | Field | Value |
    |---|---|
    | **Name** | Your label, for example `Twilio US` |
    | **Account SID** | Your Twilio Account SID |
    | **Auth Token** | Your Twilio Auth Token |

    <Check>"BYOT added successfully." appears and the account is available in the telephony account selector.</Check>

    Saving also creates a Ringg app in your Twilio account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add and use numbers

With your Twilio account selected in **Add number**, both **Import your own** and **Buy new** are available. Outbound calls need no setup on the number. For inbound, Ringg links the number to its app when you [attach it to an agent](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/inbound-agent-number) and unlinks it when you detach it. Importing, attaching and testing the numbers are the same for every provider: steps 3 and 4 of [How BYOT works](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/your-own-telephony/byot-overview#how-byot-works).

## Troubleshooting

The account, import, no-response and audio problems common to every provider are in [BYOT troubleshooting](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/your-own-telephony/byot-overview#troubleshooting).
