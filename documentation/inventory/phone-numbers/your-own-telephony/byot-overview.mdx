Bring your own telephony (<Tooltip tip="Bring your own telephony: use your existing provider account and numbers with Ringg.">BYOT</Tooltip>) lets your agents call and answer on numbers from your own carrier account instead of numbers bought through Ringg. Use it when you already have numbers, rates or compliance set up with a carrier; if you don't, [buying a Ringg-provided number](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/buy-or-import-a-number#buy-a-new-number) from Plivo or Vobiz needs no provider account.

In the dashboard: **Numbers → Add number**, then pick the provider and open **Configure provider** (the gear).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the provider side">
    Get the credentials. For providers that route calls through an app or flow (Exotel, Ozonetel), configure Ringg's URLs there first; each provider guide lists them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the account in Ringg">
    Open **Configure provider** for the provider, select **Add**, fill the fields and **Save**. See [Connect a provider account](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/buy-or-import-a-number#connect-a-provider-account-first).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import the numbers">
    With the account selected, use **Import your own**, **Buy new** (providers that sell numbers) or **Add custom** (providers that don't share a number list). See [Import a number](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/buy-or-import-a-number#import-a-number-you-already-own).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use them">
    [Attach a number to an inbound agent](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/inbound-agent-number), or pick it as caller ID for [calls](/api-reference/endpoint/calling/initiate-individual-call) and [campaigns](/documentation/deploy/campaigns/create-a-campaign).

    <Check>Place a test call in each direction you use. It appears in [Logs](/documentation/monitor/logs).</Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

These apply to every provider account; provider-specific causes are on each provider's page.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The account won't save">
    Ringg tests the credentials with most providers when you save. "Invalid credentials for _provider_" means the provider refused them: re-copy them from the provider's console without extra spaces, and check the account isn't suspended and has API access. "Missing required fields for _provider_" names the empty fields.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Numbers don't appear under Import your own">
    Check the numbers are active and voice-enabled with the provider, and that you selected the right account in **Add number**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Calls connect but the agent doesn't respond">
    Check the number is attached to the right agent and the agent is live. Open the call in [Logs](/documentation/monitor/logs) for its status and transcript, and compare it with the provider's call logs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Poor audio or dropped calls">
    Test from a second phone and network and check the provider's service status. If it persists, contact Ringg support with the Ringg call ID.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
