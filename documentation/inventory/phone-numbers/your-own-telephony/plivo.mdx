Connect your own Plivo account when you already have Plivo numbers or rates you want to keep; your agents then call and answer on those numbers like any other [number](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/overview). This is one of the [bring-your-own-telephony](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/your-own-telephony/byot-overview) options. You don't need a Plivo account to use Plivo numbers: in **Add number**, Plivo also offers Ringg-provided numbers you [buy through Ringg](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/buy-or-import-a-number#buy-a-new-number).

## Connect the account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Plivo account form">
    Go to **Numbers → Add number**, select **Plivo**, and open **Configure provider** (the gear). Select **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields and save">
    | Field | Value |
    |---|---|
    | **Name** | Your label, for example `Plivo India` |
    | **Auth ID** | Your Plivo Auth ID |
    | **Auth Token** | Your Plivo Auth Token |
    | **App ID** | Optional. Leave it blank: Ringg creates its own app in your Plivo account when you save. |

    <Check>"BYOT added successfully." appears and the account is available in the telephony account selector.</Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add and use numbers

With your Plivo account selected in **Add number**, both **Import your own** and **Buy new** are available. Outbound calls need no setup on the number. For inbound, Ringg links the number to its app when you [attach it to an agent](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/inbound-agent-number) and unlinks it when you detach it. Importing, attaching and testing the numbers are the same for every provider: steps 3 and 4 of [How BYOT works](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/your-own-telephony/byot-overview#how-byot-works).

## Troubleshooting

The account, import, no-response and audio problems common to every provider are in [BYOT troubleshooting](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/your-own-telephony/byot-overview#troubleshooting).
