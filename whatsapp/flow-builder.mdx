You can build a [WhatsApp Flow](/whatsapp/flows) in Ringg instead of Meta's WhatsApp Manager. The builder works like Meta's: screens on the left, their content in the middle, and on the right a preview you can fill in like the customer would. Ringg saves the Flow to your WhatsApp Business account as a draft, and when you publish it, any of your WhatsApp assistants can send it.

There are three ways to start:

| Start from | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Builder** | Forms such as lead capture, feedback, site visit requests or KYC details. |
| **Booking preset** | Appointment booking with free times from Calendly, Google Calendar or Cal.com. See [Booking preset](#booking-preset). |
| **Flow JSON** | Anything the builder doesn't offer. Paste or write Meta's Flow JSON. See [See and edit the Flow JSON](#see-and-edit-the-flow-json). |

## Before you start

- A connected WhatsApp Business account. See [Connect WhatsApp Business](/whatsapp/onboarding).
- You don't need access to WhatsApp Manager.

## Build a Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the builder">
    Go to **Integrations → WhatsApp Business**, open your account, select the **Flows** tab, then **Create Flow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    - **Name**: something your team will recognise, such as *Site visit request*.
    - **Category**: the closest match, for example *Lead generation* or *Appointment booking*.
    - **Start from**: **Builder**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add screens and content">
    The first screen starts with an empty **Short answer**. Select a row to edit it, use the arrows to reorder it, and the bin to remove it.

    - **Add content** adds text or a question to the screen. See [What you can add](#what-you-can-add).
    - **Add new** under **Screens** adds another screen. Give each screen a **Screen title**.

    Keep it short. Two or three screens with a handful of questions each gets the most submissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the buttons">
    Every screen ends with a **Button**:

    - On each screen except the last, it takes the customer to the next screen. It says *Continue* unless you change it.
    - On the last screen, it submits the form and sends every answer back into the chat, labelled by question. For example: *Request visit*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it in the preview">
    Fill in the preview on the right and tap its buttons to move between screens, as the customer would. Required questions you leave empty are flagged. Select a screen name above the preview to jump to it.

    When you submit, the preview shows the answers and, under **Your assistant reads**, the exact message your assistant gets:

    ```text
    User submitted the WhatsApp Flow "Site visit request": Full name=Priya Sharma,
    Phone=9876543210, Project=Green Acres Villas, Preferred visit date=2026-10-04
    ```

    Select **Start again** to clear it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the draft">
    Select **Save draft**. Ringg creates the Flow on your WhatsApp account as a draft and opens its details.

    If Meta finds a problem, the builder stays open and lists it under *Meta found problems in the saved draft*. The draft is saved, but it can't be published until you fix the problem and save again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    In the details, check the preview once more, select **Publish**, then **Publish** again to confirm.

    <Warning>
    Published Flows can't be changed. To change one later, see [Change a published Flow](#change-a-published-flow).
    </Warning>

    The Flow is now ready to [add to an assistant](/whatsapp/flows#let-your-assistant-send-it) or to [a template's Flow button](/whatsapp/flows#open-a-flow-from-a-message-template).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can add

**Add content** lists the same components as WhatsApp Manager. The character limits are Meta's, and the builder shows how many you've used.

| Group | Content | What the customer gets | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text | **Large heading**, **Small heading** | A title inside the screen | 80 characters |
| | **Body** | A paragraph of text | 4,096 characters |
| | **Caption** | Small print | 409 characters |
| Text answer | **Short answer** | A one-line field. Choose its **Input type**: *Text*, *Email*, *Number* or *Phone*. | Label up to 20 characters |
| | **Paragraph** | A field for a few lines, such as an address | Label up to 20 characters |
| | **Date picker** | A calendar to pick a date | Label up to 40 characters |
| Selection | **Single choice** | A list where they pick one | Up to 20 options. Label up to 30 characters. |
| | **Multiple choice** | A list where they pick one or more | Up to 20 options. Label up to 30 characters. |
| | **Dropdown** | A menu where they pick one | Up to 200 options. Label up to 20 characters. |
| | **Opt-in** | A checkbox they tick, such as consent to be contacted | Up to 120 characters |

Each question also has:

- **Label**: what the customer sees, and the name its answer comes back under.
- **Required**: on by default. The customer can't continue until they answer.
- **Instructions** (optional): a hint under the field, such as *As on your ID*. Up to 80 characters. For short answers, paragraphs and date pickers.
- **Options**, for choices and dropdowns: each has a title of up to 30 characters and an optional description of up to 300.

A screen holds up to 49 pieces of content, and a button label is up to 35 characters.

<Tip>
Labels matter. Ringg shows the customer's answers under these labels in the chat, in **Logs** and in your webhooks, and your assistant reads them too. *Preferred visit date* is clearer than *Date*.
</Tip>

## See and edit the Flow JSON

Every Flow is a Flow JSON document that WhatsApp reads. Select the **JSON** tab in the builder to see the complete Flow JSON for what you've built. It updates as you edit. Developers can read it, or copy it into WhatsApp Manager.

To use something the builder doesn't offer, such as images, screens that depend on earlier answers, or other components from Meta's [Flow JSON reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/flows/reference/flowjson):

1. In the **JSON** tab, select **Edit JSON**, then **Edit JSON** again to confirm.
2. Edit the JSON in the code editor. The preview follows your changes.
3. Select **Save draft**.

This is one way: once a Flow is edited as JSON, it can't go back to the builder.

To start from JSON, for example one a developer wrote, choose **Start from → Flow JSON** when you create the Flow and paste it in. Ringg sends it to Meta exactly as it is.

<Note>
In a Flow you write as JSON, only the values in the last screen's `complete` action come back into the chat. Add every answer you need to its `payload`, such as `"full_name": "${form.full_name}"`. The builder does this for you.
</Note>

The preview draws text, text fields, choices, dropdowns, date pickers and opt-ins. Other components show only their name, such as *Image*. To see them, save the draft and select **Open preview** in its details for Meta's own preview.

## Drafts

Open a draft from the **Flows** tab to see its details. Its buttons are:

- **Edit**: reopens it in the builder.
- **Publish**: makes it ready to send.
- **Delete draft**: removes it from your WhatsApp account. Only drafts can be deleted.

Drafts can't be sent to customers. In an assistant's Flow picker, a draft is greyed out with *Draft: publish it from the Flows tab*.

## Change a published Flow

Meta locks a Flow once it's published, so you change a copy instead:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy it">
    Open the Flow in the **Flows** tab:

    - For a Flow built in Ringg, select **Duplicate to edit**. The copy opens in the builder.
    - For a Flow made in WhatsApp Manager, select **Copy to edit in Ringg**. The copy opens as Flow JSON.

    The copy is a new draft named after the original, with *(copy)* at the end. Rename it if you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit, save and publish the copy">
    Make your changes, select **Save draft**, then publish it from its details. If the original used [live data](/whatsapp/flows-live-data), set it up again on the copy before publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to the copy">
    - In each assistant that sent the original, remove the old Flow under **WhatsApp Flows**, add the copy, and update its `@` tag in the prompt.
    - A template's Flow button keeps opening the original. Edit the template to use the copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Booking preset

The booking preset is a ready-made appointment Flow. The customer picks a date, then a free time from your calendar, then enters their name and email. You don't design anything.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Select **Create Flow**, name it, for example *Book a clinic visit*, choose the category **Appointment booking**, and set **Start from** to **Booking preset**. Select **Save draft**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a calendar">
    In the Flow's details, under **Live data**, select **Set up live data**, choose **A calendar**, pick your connected calendar and select **Save**. The screens are already filled in for you. For the other settings, see [Flows with live data](/whatsapp/flows-live-data#set-up-appointment-booking).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish it">
    Select **Publish**, then confirm. **Publish** stays greyed out until a calendar is connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let your assistant book">
    Add the Flow and the calendar's booking tool to your assistant, as in [Let your assistant send it and book](/whatsapp/flows-live-data#let-your-assistant-send-it-and-book).
  </Step>
</Steps>

In the builder's preview, **See times** can't load times, because only WhatsApp can ask the calendar. Select **2. Pick a time** above the preview to see the next screen, or try the whole Flow with **Open preview** once it's saved.

## Troubleshooting

The builder names the first problem it finds at the bottom left, next to **Save draft**, which stays greyed out until it's fixed.

| You see | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Give the Flow a name.* or *Give screen 2 a title.* | Builder | Fill in the **Name** or the **Screen title**. |
| *Every question on screen 2 needs a label.* | Builder | Fill in the question's **Label**, or remove the question. |
| *"Preferred date of the site visit" is longer than 40 characters.* | Builder | Shorten it. The limits are Meta's. See [What you can add](#what-you-can-add). |
| *"Project" needs at least one option.* | Builder | Add an option, or remove the question. |
| *Add some content to screen 2.* | Builder | Add a question or text, or delete the screen with its bin. |
| *Meta found problems in the saved draft* | Top of the builder | The draft is saved, but Meta lists what it won't accept. Fix each one and select **Save draft** again. |
| *Not valid JSON* or *The Flow JSON needs a screens list.* | Flow JSON editor | Fix the JSON. Every Flow needs a `screens` list. |
| *Could not save the Flow* | A pop-up message | The reason is shown under it. Fix it and save again. |
| *Connect a calendar under Live data to publish.* | Details of a booking preset Flow | [Connect a calendar](#booking-preset) first. |
| *This step asks the Flow's endpoint for the next screen* | Builder preview | Steps that load live data only run in WhatsApp. Select the next screen above the preview, or use **Open preview** after saving. |

## Good to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Drafts also appear in WhatsApp Manager">
    A Flow built in Ringg lives on your WhatsApp Business account, like one made in WhatsApp Manager, so you can see it there too. If you change a draft in WhatsApp Manager, saving it again in Ringg replaces those changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How answers are named">
    Each answer comes back under its question's label, such as *Full name=Priya Sharma*. If you change a label in a copy, update any prompt or analysis that mentions the old one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Flows belong to one WhatsApp Business account">
    A Flow is created on the account whose **Flows** tab you build it in. Only assistants with a number on that account can send it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
