Every member of a Ringg Analytics workspace has one role. The role decides which calls they can open and which pages they see; the server enforces the same rules, so a hidden page's data can't be fetched another way.

In [Ringg Analytics](https://analytics.ringg.ai): **Sidebar → Team** (owners and admins).

## Roles

| Role | Calls they see | Pages | Can also |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | All | All | Everything an admin can. Set when the workspace is created. |
| **Admin** | All | All, plus **Team** and **Settings** | Manage members, agents, metrics, notifications and dispute settings; reassign disputes; delete and reprocess calls |
| **QA** | All | All except **Team** and **Settings** | Add calls, [review](/analytics-platform/review-calls/qa-reviews) calls, resolve [disputes](/analytics-platform/disputes/review-a-dispute), set up notifications, coach agents |
| **Team lead** | Their team: the agents who report to them, directly or further down | **Summary** (their team), **Interactions**, **Agents** (their team, read-only), **Metrics** (read-only) | Leave coaching notes on their agents' [report cards](/analytics-platform/coach-agents/report-cards) |
| **Agent** | Their own | **Summary** (their own), **Interactions**, **My report card**, **Metrics** (read-only) | [Dispute](/analytics-platform/disputes/raise-a-dispute) an AI score |
| **Member** | All | All except **Notifications**, **Team** and **Settings** | Read only |

A team lead sees the workspace average as a benchmark, but never another team's figures. An agent sees no team or workspace figures at all.

## Invite members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite QAs, admins and members from Team">
    Click **Invite User**, enter the name and email, and pick the role. For a QA, also pick the **Review mode** and whether they must **Rate and comment on every parameter** (see [QA settings](#qa-settings)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add agents and team leads from Agents">
    Adding an agent with an email invites them and links their login to their agent record, which is what limits them to their own calls. See [Agents and teams](/analytics-platform/coach-agents/agents-and-teams).

    <Check>The invite arrives by email, and the member shows **Invite pending** until they sign in.</Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

An agent or team lead whose login isn't linked to an agent record sees no calls. Their **Summary** says "Your login isn't linked to an agent yet".

## QA settings

Set per QA when you invite them, or later from the icon next to their role on **Team**.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Review mode: Blind review** (recommended) | The QA scores calls without seeing the AI. AI scores, reasoning and summaries show as `***`, and pages built on AI scores (**Summary**, **Business**, **Agents**, **Analytics**, **Notifications**) are hidden. |
| **Review mode: Full view** | The QA sees AI scores, reasoning and summaries next to the rubric, plus the dashboards. |
| **Rate and comment on every parameter** | The QA can't save a review until every parameter has a rating and a comment. |
