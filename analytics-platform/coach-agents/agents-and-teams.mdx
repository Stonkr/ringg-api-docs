**Agents** holds one record per person whose calls you score, with their level and team lead. The reporting lines decide what a team lead sees: their own calls and those of everyone below them.

In [Ringg Analytics](https://analytics.ringg.ai): **Sidebar → Agents**.

## Add agents

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up levels">
    Click **Manage Levels** and add your levels, for example **Agent** and **Team Lead**. An agent at the team-lead level signs in as a team lead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add agents">
    Click **Add Agent** for one, or **Import CSV** for many. The CSV takes **Name**, **Email**, **Level** and **Reports To**; download the template from the import dialog. The whole file is checked first, and nothing is added if a row has an error.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set who they report to">
    Pick each agent's team lead under **Reports to**, or drag them onto their team lead in the org chart (the second view switch at the top).

    <Check>An agent added with an email gets an invite and shows **Invited**, then **Joined** once they sign in.</Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you add calls with **Bulk calls**, each row is matched to an agent by email, then by name, and an agent that doesn't exist yet is created.

## Columns

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Login** | **Joined**, **Invited**, **Not invited**, or **Add email to invite** |
| **Interactions** | Calls and emails in the period |
| **Avg score** | Average official score in the period |
| **Total HT** / **Avg HT** | Total and average handling time |

Click an agent to open their [report card](/analytics-platform/coach-agents/report-cards). **Export** downloads the table.

## What team leads see

A team lead's **Agents** page is titled **Your team**. It lists only their team, read-only: no **Add Agent**, **Import CSV**, **Manage Levels** or **Export**, and reporting lines can't be changed. Clicking a row opens that agent's report card.
