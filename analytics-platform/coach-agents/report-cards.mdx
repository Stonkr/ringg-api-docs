A report card is one agent's performance over a period: how their score moved, which parameters cost them points, and the calls behind it. Team leads, QAs and admins leave coaching notes there, and the agent reads them on their own report card.

In [Ringg Analytics](https://analytics.ringg.ai): **Sidebar → Agents →** click an agent. Agents open their own from **Sidebar → My report card**.

## What's on a report card

| Section | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Average score** | The official average, the change against the previous period, and for coaches the team and workspace averages |
| **Scored calls**, **Fatal calls**, **Appeals** | Calls in the period, fatal ones, and [disputes](/analytics-platform/disputes/overview) raised and how many changed the score |
| **Score trend** | Average score per day for periods up to two weeks, per week beyond (India time) |
| **Parameters** | For each parameter, the share of its points the agent earned, the change, and the team's share. The tick on each bar is the workspace average. **Needs work** lists parameters under 80% on at least 3 calls, and any fatal miss. |
| **Focus areas** | Up to three parameters that need work and three strengths, from parameters scored on at least 3 calls |
| **Recent calls** | The latest calls with their official score and dispute result. **All calls** opens **Interactions** filtered to the agent. |
| **Coaching** | Notes from the agent's team lead, QAs and admins, newest first |

Scores are official: a resolved dispute's QA score replaces the AI's. Parameters marked N/A or optional on a call don't count toward it.

## Coaching notes

| | Who can |
|---|---|
| Write a note | The agent's team lead, QAs, admins and the owner. Nobody can coach themselves. |
| Mark a note read | Only the agent, with **Mark as read** |
| Delete a note | Its author, or an admin |

An agent's own report card has no team or workspace figures, and no note box.
