When an agent appeals a call, a QA re-scores it. You rate each disputed parameter with a remark the agent reads, keep or change the AI's ratings for the rest, and resolve; your score becomes the call's official score.

In [Ringg Analytics](https://analytics.ringg.ai): **Sidebar → Interactions → Filter → Appeal**. Owners, admins and QAs can review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the queue">
    On **Interactions**, click **Filter → Appeal**, pick **Waiting for review** and click **Save**. The queue lists the oldest appeal first.

    <Frame caption="Appeals waiting for review">
      <img src="/media/disputes-review/queue.webp" alt="Interactions with the Appeal: Waiting for review filter chip, listing three calls by Meera Iyer, Rahul Verma and Vikram Rao, each with an Appeal · waiting badge" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim the appeal">
    Click the call, then **Open full page**. The **Appeal** card shows who appealed, their team lead, the **QA target** and the disputed parameters. Click **Start review**.

    <Frame caption="An appeal waiting for a QA">
      <img src="/media/disputes-review/start-review.webp" alt="Appeal card marked Submitted: submitted by Meera Iyer with team lead Rohan Das, QA target with 6 hours left, 1 disputed parameter, and a Start review button" />
    </Frame>

    <Check>The appeal moves to **In review**, and other QAs see that you have it.</Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rate the disputed parameters">
    **Review the appeal** opens with "Disputed by the agent" first. Each disputed parameter shows the agent's reason and the AI's. Pick your rating and write a remark: the remark is required, and the agent reads it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep or change the rest">
    **Other parameters** holds everything nobody disputed. Click **Keep AI ratings for …** to keep the AI's rating on all of them, or **Rate them myself** to open them and change any. Tick **Mark interaction as fatal** if the call breaks a fatal rule, and add a note to the agent if you want; it goes in their email.

    <Frame caption="A disputed parameter accepted, the AI's ratings kept for the rest">

    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve">
    Check **Score so far** and click **Resolve appeal**. The confirmation says how many parameters keep the AI's rating; click **Submit final score**, or **Back** to change something.

    <Frame caption="The last check before the score is final">

Your work saves as a draft while you review, so you can leave and come back. Only reviewers see the draft; the agent never does.

## Queue filters

| Filter | Lists |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for review** | Appeals no QA has claimed |
| **In review** | Appeals a QA has claimed |
| **In review by me** | Appeals you have claimed |
| **Overdue** | Open appeals past their **QA target** |
| **Resolved** | Appeals with a final score |
| **Not appealed** | Calls without an appeal |

## Release or reassign

- Click **Release** to put an appeal you claimed back in the queue.
- An owner or admin can **Take back** anyone's claim, or pick a QA under **Assign to…** to hand it over.
- A reviewer who is removed from the workspace, or moved to a role that can't review, gives up their claims automatically.

## Blind review

A QA in **Blind review** sees the names of the disputed parameters but not the agent's reasons, and none of the AI's ratings or reasons, so they re-score the call from the recording. **Keep AI ratings** still works; the ratings stay hidden. See [QA settings](/analytics-platform/roles-and-access#qa-settings).

## Outcomes

Each disputed parameter is marked **Changed** or **Unchanged** (**Accepted** or **Rejected** in full view), and the appeal as a whole:

| Outcome | When |
|---|---|
| **AI score upheld** | You changed none of the disputed parameters |
| **Partially overturned** | You changed some of them |
| **Overturned** | You changed all of them |
