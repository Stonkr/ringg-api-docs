When a QA resolves a dispute, the result shows on the call for the agent, their team lead and reviewers, and an email goes out. If the decision has to change later, a QA corrects it; every version is kept.

In [Ringg Analytics](https://analytics.ringg.ai): **Sidebar → Interactions →** open the call.

## What the agent sees

| Part | Shows |
|---|---|
| "Final score · Appeal reviewed by QA" | The official score, the AI's score and the difference, and the outcome: **AI score upheld**, **Partially overturned** or **Overturned** |
| **Appeal** card | Status, who submitted it and when, who resolved it and when |
| Disputed parameters | The AI's rating, the QA's, **Changed** or **Unchanged**, and the QA's remark |
| The AI's score | The original AI scoring, unchanged, for comparison |

The agent and their current team lead get an email: "Appeal reviewed: … — final score N%", with each disputed parameter's result and the QA's note.

The official score then counts everywhere: **Interactions**, **Summary**, **Agents**, [report cards](/analytics-platform/coach-agents/report-cards), [notification](/analytics-platform/set-up/notifications) conditions and exports.

## Appeal history

**Appeal history** at the bottom of the call lists every step: **Appealed**, **Review started**, **Released**, **Reassigned**, **Resolved** and **Corrected**, by whom and when, with each version's final score. Everyone who can see the call can read it, and it is never deleted.

## Corrections

Use a correction when a resolved decision turns out wrong, for example after a team lead escalates it offline and the escalation is accepted.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the resolved call">
    As a QA, admin or owner, open the call and click **Correct the final score (offline dispute accepted)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-rate and give a reason">
    Change the ratings that need it and write why you are correcting. The reason is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Review correction**, then **Save correction**.

    <Check>The call shows the new final score and **Corrected · v2**, with your reason. The agent and their team lead get an email: "Score corrected: … — now N%".</Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="A corrected dispute">
  <img src="/media/disputes-corrections/corrected.webp" alt="Final score 86, AI scored 71 (+15) · Overturned · corrected (v2); the Appeal card marked Resolved and Corrected · v2; both disputed parameters marked Changed; the correction reason; and the Correct the final score (offline dispute accepted) button" />
</Frame>

If two reviewers correct the same appeal at once, the second save is refused, so nobody overwrites a correction they haven't seen.
