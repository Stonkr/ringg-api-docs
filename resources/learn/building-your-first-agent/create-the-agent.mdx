**Part 2: Building your first agent · Lesson 1 of 4 · 10 minutes**

## Objective

Create an outbound agent from a template and open it in the editor, ready to be given a prompt.

## The scenario

Acme Lending sends a reminder call three days before a customer's EMI is due. The agent, Ananya, confirms who she is speaking to, states the amount and date, and asks when the customer will pay. This lesson creates her; the next three give her a prompt, a voice and a test call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the create dialog">
    In the sidebar, click **Assistants**, then **Create agent**. In the **Create Assistant** dialog, choose the **Outbound** tab: the reminder is a call Acme places, not one it receives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start from the Financial templates">
    Under **Select industry templates**, click **Financial** and pick the template closest to a payment reminder. A template gives you a prompt written for the use case, which you edit in the next lesson.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the agent and pick its languages">
    Set **Agent Name** to `EMI reminder - Hindi`. The name is how your team finds the agent later, so say what it does and in which language.

    Set **Primary Language** to English (India) and **Secondary Language** to Hindi, so Ananya can switch when a customer answers in Hindi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a voice">
    Pick any Indian English female voice; clicking a card plays its preview. You will compare voices properly in lesson 3. Click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the call context">
    These fields are what the template turns into a prompt. Enter:

    - **Company details**: `Acme Lending offers personal and two-wheeler loans across Maharashtra.`
    - **Purpose of the call**: `Remind the customer of their upcoming EMI and note the date they will pay.`

    Leave the custom variables and knowledge base as they are; part 3 covers both. Click **Create Assistant**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the prompt">
    A progress screen shows while Ringg writes the prompt from your context, usually under a minute. Then you see **Assistant created** and the agent opens in the editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoint

<Check>
The editor's top bar shows **EMI reminder - Hindi** and a **Test assistant** button, and the **Prompt** section holds a first message and prompt sections. The agent also appears on the **Agents** page.

## Exercise

Open **Create agent** again, pick the same Financial template, fill in **Agent basics**, click **Next**, then click **Back**. Expected result: the first step still shows the name, languages and voice you entered; **Back** returns without losing them. Close the dialog without creating a second agent.

## What you learned

- Every agent starts in the **Create Assistant** dialog, where the channel tab decides whether it calls, answers, runs on a web page or on WhatsApp.
- A template writes the first prompt from the call context you enter. You edit from there rather than from a blank page.
- Languages and voice are set at creation but can be changed later, so a wrong choice here costs nothing.
