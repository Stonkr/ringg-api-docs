**Part 2: Building your first agent · Lesson 3 of 4 · 10 minutes**

## Objective

Compare voices properly, hear Ananya in both of her languages, pick a speaking speed, and confirm Hindi stays her secondary language.

## The scenario

Acme's callers speak English or Hindi, and Ananya reads out an amount and a date on every call. Lesson 1 picked a voice in a hurry; this is where the choice gets made.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Voice section">
    In the agent editor, click **Voice**. It has three parts, **Language**, **Voice** and **Voice speed**, and one **Save** button at the bottom for all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the languages">
    **Primary Language** should read English (India) and **Secondary Language** Hindi, as set when you created the agent. Do not change the primary language now: changing it clears the secondary language and the selected voice, and you would have to pick both again.

    Leave **Add more languages** alone. Additional languages are for callers who speak languages the agent does not, and saving them switches the speech-to-text provider; Acme's callers speak English or Hindi, which are already covered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the grid">
    The **Voice** part lists voices that speak the primary or secondary language. The tabs along the top are **All** and one tab per voice provider in the list; the tag chips under them narrow it further, and combine, so a voice must have every selected tag. Click **Clear** to reset. Click **See more** if the voice you want is not in the first rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare three voices in both languages">
    Clicking a voice selects it and plays its preview. Because Ananya has a secondary language, the **Preview language** icon next to the tabs switches the preview between English and Hindi: listen to each candidate in both before deciding. A voice that cannot speak the primary language opens a **Change agent language?** dialog; click **Cancel** and pick another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the speed">
    **Voice speed** has six steps from **0.7x** to **1.2x**, with **1.0x** the default. The play button beside it previews the selected voice at that speed. Ananya reads out an amount and a date, so compare **0.9x** and **1.0x** and keep the one where "four thousand five hundred rupees" is easiest to follow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoint

<Check>
"Voice settings saved" appears, **Save** is disabled again, and the voice you chose is first in the grid.
</Check>

## Exercise

Give the speech recogniser the words it is most likely to mishear on Acme's calls. Under **Advanced Settings → Keyword Boosting**, type `Acme Lending, EMI, NACH` and click **Add**. Expected result: "Vocabulary updated successfully" appears and each word shows as a chip below the field; there is no separate **Save** for this list.

## What you learned

- Language, voice and speed are one section with one **Save**; changing the primary language clears the secondary language and the voice.
- With a secondary language, the **Preview language** icon lets you hear a voice in both languages before you commit.
- Speed runs from 0.7x to 1.2x, default 1.0x, and the play button previews the selected voice at the chosen speed.
