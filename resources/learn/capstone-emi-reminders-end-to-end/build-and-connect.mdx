**Part 7: Capstone: EMI reminders end to end · Lesson 2 of 4 · 20 minutes**

## Objective

Clear checkpoints 1 to 3: the agent answers from Acme's policies, reads each loan's dues from the CRM before it dials, and calls from a workspace number.

## The scenario

Ananya exists from part 2, and part 3 gave her the knowledge base and the CRM tool. Two things change for production: a loan account the CRM does not know gets no call at all, rather than a call with no amount in it, and calls come from Acme's own number.

## Hands-on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Checkpoint 1: knowledge attached and mentioned">
    Click **Assistants** and open `EMI reminder - Hindi` (if it is gone, [create it again](/documentation/build-agents/agents/create-an-agent) and redo part 3). Click [**Knowledge Base**](/documentation/build-agents/customize-agent-behaviour/attach-a-knowledge-base) in the editor sidebar. The EMI policy knowledge base should be listed below **Select Knowledge Base**; if not, click the button and tick it: "Knowledge base attached successfully". Attachments belong to the version you are editing, so check the version that will take the calls.

    Open **Prompt** and find the `@` mention that tells Ananya to answer late-fee and grace-period questions from the knowledge base. A red mention means it was detached; attaching it again fixes it. No mention: type `@`, pick it under **Knowledge Bases**, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Checkpoint 2: CRM lookup that blocks unknown accounts">
    Open **Advanced Settings → Tools**, then **Pre-call**. Open `crm_lookup` and click **Test API**: the **Response** tab shows the CRM's reply and the response time. Keep only the fields the prompt uses ticked.

    On **Settings**, confirm `404` is still under **Block call on status codes** from part 3; without it, a CRM miss only skips the tool's data and Ananya dials without the dues. Click **Save tool** if you changed anything. In **Prompt**, the first message should read the dues as `@((crm_lookup.<field>))`, with the field names your CRM returns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Checkpoint 3: a workspace number to call from">
    Click **Test assistant**. Under **Choose a number**, pick the number from part 4, listed under **Your numbers**; **Test numbers** are for testing only. Fill in the variables and call your own phone, as in [part 2, lesson 4](/resources/learn/building-your-first-agent/test-it).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoint

<Check>
**Knowledge Base** lists the EMI policy knowledge base and its mention in the prompt is not red. The **Response** tab of `crm_lookup` shows the CRM's reply. Your phone shows your workspace number as the caller, Ananya's first sentence has the amount and date the CRM returned, and the call appears in Logs.
</Check>

## Exercise

Click the trash icon on the attached knowledge base ("Knowledge base detached successfully"), then open **Prompt**. Expected result: the `@` mention turns red. Tick the knowledge base again under **Select Knowledge Base** and the mention returns to normal.

## What you learned

- A checkpoint is proved, not assumed: the attached list, the **Response** tab and the Logs entry are the proof.
- **Block call on status codes** turns a CRM miss into no call. Other errors let the call go out without data, the worse outcome for a reminder.
- The outbound caller ID is chosen per test call and per campaign. **Attach Number** is for agents that answer calls; an outbound single-prompt agent never shows it.
