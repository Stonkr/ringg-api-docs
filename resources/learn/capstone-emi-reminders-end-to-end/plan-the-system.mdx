**Part 7: Capstone: EMI reminders end to end · Lesson 1 of 4 · 10 minutes**

## Objective

Say what the finished system does, name its six checkpoints, and know which already exist in your workspace.

## The scenario

Acme Lending wants the reminder system live for the 15 October EMI run. Ananya has to call each customer in English or Hindi, answer questions about late fees, know the loan's dues before she dials, record whether the customer promised to pay and when, hand that to the CRM, respect a limit on how often one person is called, and leave the collections lead a report. Parts 2 to 6 built those pieces on the same agent. The capstone checks each on screen, adds the CRM webhook and the call limit, then runs the campaign.

## Hands-on

  <Step title="Know the checkpoints">
    A checkpoint is done when its proof is on screen.

    | # | Checkpoint | You are done when | Lesson |

    | 1 | Agent with knowledge | **Knowledge Base** on `EMI reminder - Hindi` lists the EMI policy knowledge base, and the prompt mentions it | 2 |
    | 2 | CRM lookup | `crm_lookup` is on the **Pre-call** tab, **Test API** returns the CRM's reply, and the first message reads the dues from it | 2 |
    | 3 | A number to call from | A test call from one of **Your numbers** reaches your phone and appears in Logs | 2 |
    | 4 | Webhook to the CRM | A subscription card shows your URL and `all_processing_completed`, and your server logs one request per finished call | 3 |
    | 5 | Call limit | **Limit how often one person is called** is on and saved | 4 |

## Exercise

Write down what Acme's CRM needs per call to record a promise to pay. Expected result: `loan_account`, the three Advanced Analysis fields (`payment_promised`, `promised_date`, `objection`) and a reference to the call. Lesson 3 shows where each sits in the webhook request.

## What you learned

- The system is six checkpoints across one agent, one number, one subscription, one limit and one campaign; the capstone adds wiring, not features.
- A checkpoint counts only when its proof is on screen: the attached list, the tool's response, the Logs entry, the subscription card.
- Checkpoints 4 to 6 are new for everyone; 1 to 3 depend on how far you took parts 3 and 4.
