**Part 7: Capstone: EMI reminders end to end · Lesson 4 of 4 · 20 minutes**

## Objective

Run the reminder campaign under a call limit, then read connectivity, promises to pay and the exported report.

## The scenario

The October run goes to every customer with an EMI due on 15 October, under two rules from compliance: nobody is called more than three times in a day, retries included, and the collections lead gets a report. You run it over the part 4 list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Checkpoint 5: set the call limit">
    Open **Settings → [Call limits](/documentation/manage-account/call-limits)** and switch on **Limit how often one person is called**. Set **Maximum calls to one person** to `3` and **Rolling window** to 24 hours; **Projected impact** shows what those values would have held back. Click **Save**, then **Save limit** in the **This affects calls already queued** dialog.

    The limit counts every agent's calls to the same person, retries included, never test or inbound calls. A campaign call over it waits as **Waiting for limit** in Logs; if no slot opens before the campaign's end time, it is cancelled instead and shows **Cancelled by limit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload details">
    Open **Monitor → Campaigns → Create campaign**. Name it `October EMI reminders`, pick `EMI reminder - Hindi` under **Select assistant**, upload the part 4 CSV, check the mapping for `due_amount`, `due_date` and `loan_account`, and click **Next**: "CSV verified successfully".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure campaign">
    Under **Select number**, pick the part 4 pool under **Number pool**. Keep the default **Start date & time**, 10 minutes from now, and set **End date & time** to later today. **Calling windows** default to 08:00–20:00 in the campaign's **Timezone**; click **Edit** if you are outside them now.

    Set **Retry count** to **2 Retries**: with the first attempt, that is three dials per row, which fits the limit; with the default **3 Retries**, the limit would hold back or cancel a fourth. Set **Cooldown Period** to **5 Minutes**. The read-only **Call limits** card confirms the limit applies. Click **Create campaign**: "Campaign created successfully!". Every attempt is a real call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it run">
    Answer when Ananya calls, and let one row ring out to see its retry 5 minutes later. Click the campaign's row, then [**History**](/documentation/deploy/campaigns/manage-a-campaign#find-campaign-calls-in-logs): Logs opens filtered to this campaign, whatever the date range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Checkpoint 6: read the report">
    In the detail panel click **Analytics**; the page is scoped to the campaign. Read **Calls connected** and **Connectivity rate**; **Goal achieved**, the share of analysed calls where `payment_promised` was true; and the **Connectivity** chart by attempt, which decides next month's retry count.

    Once the status is **Completed**, the panel shows **Export report**. Click **Download** ("Download a ZIP of the call report"). The dashboard offers it for 60 days after the campaign's end time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoint

<Check>
**Call limits** shows the switch on with 3 calls in 24 hours. `October EMI reminders` is **Completed**, **History** lists every row's calls, Analytics shows **Goal achieved** as a percentage rather than "Pick an agent to see goal metrics", and the ZIP is in your downloads.
</Check>

## Exercise

In Analytics for the campaign, click the **Calls connected** value. Expected result: Logs opens filtered to status **Completed** and this campaign: only the calls you answered.

## What you learned

- The call limit is a workspace rule counted across agents and retries; a campaign's retry count has to fit inside it, or the extra attempts wait or are cancelled.
- Results live in Logs per call (**History**), Analytics for the rates, and the ZIP (**Export report**), offered for 60 days after the campaign ends.
- **Goal achieved** says whether the reminder worked; it exists because part 3 marked a Boolean as the goal.
