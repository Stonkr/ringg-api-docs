**Part 7: Capstone: EMI reminders end to end · Lesson 3 of 4 · 20 minutes**

## Objective

Have Ringg send every finished call to your CRM endpoint as one request, and know which fields to store.

## The scenario

After each call, Acme's CRM needs to know, per loan account, whether the customer promised to pay and for when, with a link to the recording for disputes. In part 6 you read that from Logs by hand; from now on the CRM gets it on its own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Event Subscription">
    Open `EMI reminder - Hindi`, then **Advanced Settings → Event Subscription**. With no subscriptions yet, the **New subscription** form is already open; otherwise click **Create Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point it at the CRM">
    Leave **Method** on **POST** and set **Callback URL** to your endpoint; Acme's is `https://crm.acme-lending.example/webhooks/ringg`. It must use HTTPS and return a `2xx` status within 30 seconds, so store the request first and process it later ([endpoint requirements](/documentation/build-agents/customize-agent-behaviour/webhooks/setup-and-errors#endpoint-requirements)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the event">
    Under **Select Events**, tick **All Processing Done** only. It fires once the recording, transcript and both analyses are ready and carries all of them, so the CRM gets one request per call. **Call Completed** fires for every attempt, busy and unanswered included, with no analysis yet; add it, or **Call Started**, only for live progress.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the secret header">
    Under **Custom Headers**, click **Add Header** and enter `Authorization` with the value `Bearer <your-token>`. Ringg sends it with every request, so your server can reject requests without it. After saving, the value is masked (`sk_l***`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and fire it">
    Click **Create Subscription**: "Event subscription added", and a card with the **Webhook URL**, **Events** and **Headers**. Place a test call to your phone, answer, and say you will pay on 15 October.
  </Step>
</Steps>

By API, the same subscription is one `PATCH /agent/v1` with `operation: "edit_event_subscriptions"`; see [Configure subscriptions](/documentation/build-agents/customize-agent-behaviour/webhooks/setup-and-errors#configure-subscriptions).

### The request your CRM receives

The `all_processing_completed` event for one outbound call, from [Webhook payloads](/documentation/build-agents/customize-agent-behaviour/webhooks/call-events#all-processing-completed-event). ### What to store

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| `call_id` and `event_type` | The dedupe key: the same event can arrive twice, so update rather than insert. |
| `custom_args_values.loan_account` | Which account the result belongs to. |
| `status`, `sub_status` | `completed` / `ACCEPTED` means the call connected normally; anything else has no promise to record. |
| `client_analysis` | `payment_promised`, `promised_date` and `objection`, when `client_analysis_status` is `success`. |
| `platform_analysis.summary` | One line for the relationship manager. |
| `recording_url` | The link may expire; fetch a fresh one from [Get Call Details](/api-reference/endpoint/history/get-call-details), and download the file if disputes need it. |
| `bulk_list_id` | The campaign the call belongs to; `null` for single calls. |
| `parent_call_id` | Set on a retry or callback of an earlier call; link it to that call rather than counting a second contact. |

## Checkpoint

<Check>
The subscription card shows **POST**, your URL and `all_processing_completed`. After the test call, your server has logged one request with `event_type` `all_processing_completed`, `client_analysis_status` `success`, and `payment_promised` `true` with the `promised_date` you gave in `client_analysis`.
</Check>

## Exercise

Open the card's menu (**⋮**), click **Edit**, tick **Call Started** too, and click **Update Subscription**. Place one more test call. Expected result: your server logs a `call_started` request as the call is dialled, then the usual `all_processing_completed`. Untick **Call Started** again afterwards.

## What you learned

- One event, `all_processing_completed`, carries the whole call; the earlier events are for live progress.
- Your endpoint answers `2xx` fast and deduplicates on `call_id` plus `event_type`; retries and callbacks are separate calls that point back with `parent_call_id`.
- The promise lives in `client_analysis`, under the keys you defined in Advanced Analysis.
