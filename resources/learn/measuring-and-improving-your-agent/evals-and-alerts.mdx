**Part 6: Measuring and improving your agent · Lesson 3 of 4 · 20 minutes**

## Objective

Make the promise-to-pay path a test Ananya has to pass, and get an email when her calls start failing.

## The scenario

Three transcripts you can read; three thousand you can't. Evals scores every completed call and replays one conversation path as a simulated call; Alerts watch a metric on a schedule. Ringg turns Evals on per agent: if the page says "No agents have evals enabled.", write to admin@ringg.ai with the agent ID and do the alert first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ananya's evals">
    Open **Monitor → Evals** and pick **EMI reminder - Hindi**. **Metrics** shows an average score per metric for the last 30 days, for the metrics enabled on the agent, such as **Factual accuracy**, **Instruction following**, **Knowledge coverage** and **Tool accuracy**. **Issues** groups the failures, each with **Add as test case**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the test case">
    Click **Create a test case**, choose **Voice**, and on **Write a prompt** fill in:

    - **Name**: `Promise to pay on 15 October`
    - **Conversation path**: `The customer is Rahul Verma. He confirms it is him, listens to the amount, says he will pay on 15 October, then asks whether there is a late fee if he misses that date.`
    - **Success criteria**: `The agent states the due amount and due date, confirms 15 October as the payment date, answers the late-fee question from the EMI policy, and ends the call politely.`

    Click **Add test case**. It appears with the status **Never run**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it">
    Click **Run tests**. The case moves from **Queued** to **Running**, then to **Passed**, **Failed** or **Not evaluated**. Open it for the verdict, a reason per criterion, which version ran, and the simulated conversation. **Not evaluated** means the simulation never reached the situation; make the **Conversation path** more specific and **Run again**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the alert">
    <Frame caption="Creating an alert with email and webhook notifications">
      <video alt="Creating an alert with email and webhook notifications" aria-label="Creating an alert with email and webhook notifications" controls muted playsInline className="w-full rounded-xl" style={{ aspectRatio: "16 / 10" }} preload="metadata" controlsList="nodownload noremoteplayback" poster="/media/monitor-alerts/poster.webp">
        <source src="/media/monitor-alerts/video.webm" type='video/webm; codecs="av01.0.09M.08"' />
      </video>
    </Frame>

    Open **Monitor → Alerts** and click **Create alert**. Set **Alert name** to `EMI reminder call failures` and **Scope** to **Agent**, then pick **EMI reminder - Hindi**. Build the **Trigger**: check every **15 min** for the last **1 hour**, when **Call failure rate** **is above** `20` (rate thresholds are percentages). Under **Notify via**, expand **Email**, type your address and click **Add**. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoint

<Check>
"Alert created", and on the **Agents** tab of Alerts the rule sits under **EMI reminder - Hindi** with its condition, scope and channel, switch on. In Evals, the case shows **Passed**, or **Failed** with the criteria it missed.
</Check>

## Exercise

On the **Workspace** tab, under **Recommended**, click the pencil icon on **Disconnection rate spike** rather than **Enable**, add your email under **Notify via** and save. Expected result: the alert is on with your address as its recipient, firing when the disconnection rate is above 25% over the last 30 minutes. **Enable** alone adds no recipients.

## What you learned

- Evals scores every completed call; a test case is one conversation path plus success criteria, simulated against the published version.
- **Not evaluated** is neither a pass nor a failure: the simulation never reached the situation under test.
- An alert is a sentence: check every *interval* for the last *window*, when *metric* *condition* *threshold*. It needs at least one email, Slack or webhook target.
