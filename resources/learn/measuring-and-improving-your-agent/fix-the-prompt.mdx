**Part 6: Measuring and improving your agent · Lesson 4 of 4 · 20 minutes**

## Objective

Change one thing in Ananya's prompt because the calls showed it was needed, ship it as a version, and know when to send it all the traffic.

## The scenario

Suppose the dry-run transcripts show Ananya reading `₹4,500` as "four comma five zero zero" and `15 October` as "fifteen ten", and Evals flags it under **Instruction following**. One rule in the prompt fixes it. It goes on a new version so **A/B Analytics** can show whether it helped.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick one problem">
    Open the transcript in Logs or the issue card in Evals, and choose one thing to change. A version that changes three things at once tells you nothing about which one worked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the fix from the voice rules">
    The [voice prompt rules](/documentation/build-agents/customize-agent-behaviour/agent-prompt#write-prompts-for-voice) list the usual culprits: long turns, several questions at once, no script for "no" or "call me later", no way to end, numbers read as written. Add this rule: `Say amounts in words, in rupees: ₹4,500 is "four thousand five hundred rupees". Say dates as "15 October", never "15/10".`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a version for it">
    Open **EMI reminder - Hindi**. **Create Versions** is on from part 5, so the version bar shows **V1** and **V2**. Click **+** and pick the **Base** version to copy, the one that did better in lesson 2. The new **V3** appears with a red dot: 0% traffic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and save V3">
    Click **V3** to switch to it, open **Prompt**, and paste the rule into **Response Guidelines**. Click **Save** or press Cmd/Ctrl+S: "Changes saved successfully". Add notes from V3's menu: `Amounts and dates in words`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hear it before it has traffic">
    To be sure a call runs V3 while it still has 0% traffic, copy V3's version ID from its menu and pass it as `version_id` on [Initiate Individual Call](/documentation/build-agents/test-and-improve/drafts-and-versions#call-a-specific-version-with-the-api), with `due_amount` `₹4,500` and `due_date` `15 October` in `custom_args_values`. Listen for the amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it traffic">
    Split the traffic as in [part 5, lesson 3](/resources/learn/designing-multi-step-conversations/versions-and-a-b): 70% on the current winner, 30% on V3. Once V3 is ahead in **A/B Analytics**, click **Finalize** in the sidebar row **A/B Tests Live!**, select V3 and **Confirm & Finalize**: all traffic goes to V3 and A/B testing turns off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoint

<Check>
The version bar shows **V3** with a green dot and its share. New calls in Logs carry a version chip in the **Assistant** column.
</Check>

## Exercise

In Evals, open the test case from lesson 3 and click **Run again**. Expected result: the new run's detail names which agent version ran and when it was published, so a pass is tied to V3.

## What you learned

- One change per version, so **A/B Analytics** can attribute the difference.
- The voice rules to reach for first: how to say amounts and dates, short turns, scripted "no" and "call me later", and how to end.
- **Finalize** is the end of a test: all traffic to the winner, A/B testing off.
