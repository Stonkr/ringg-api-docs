These lessons teach Ringg AI by building one thing: Acme Lending's EMI reminder system. Each lesson is 10–20 minutes, ends with something working in your workspace, and links to the reference page for the details.

By the end you will have an agent that calls customers about a due instalment, knows the company's policies, records a promise to pay, hands the result to your CRM, and reports on a campaign of thousands of calls.

## How lessons work

Every lesson has the same shape:

| Section | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | What you can do after the lesson, in one sentence. |
| **Hands-on** | The steps, with the dashboard video where there is one. The detail lives on the linked reference page. |
| **Checkpoint** | What you should see when it worked. |
| **Exercise** | One change to make on your own, with its expected result. |
| **Quiz** | One question on the lesson's objective. The feedback explains the rule either way. |

The scenario is fictional: Acme Lending, its agent Ananya, and its customers do not exist. Use your own phone number wherever a lesson asks for a callee.
