**Part 1: Getting to know Ringg AI · Lesson 3 of 3 · 15 minutes**

## Objective

Find every section of the dashboard, and know where to copy the IDs your developers will ask for.

## The scenario

Acme's team will share the dashboard: you build Ananya, a colleague uploads the calling list, and a developer wires the results into the CRM. Each needs a different section, and the developer needs IDs. This lesson walks the screen once so later lessons can say "open **Monitor → Logs**".

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the sidebar">
    The sidebar groups its items under **Monitor**, **Inventory** and **Labs**, with **Assistants** and **Workflows** above the groups. Collapse or expand it with <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>.</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>.</kbd> on Windows and Linux).

    <Frame caption="The dashboard: sidebar, top bar and the Agents page">
      <img src="/media/dashboard-tour/dashboard-shell.webp" alt="Ringg AI dashboard with the sidebar (Assistants, Workflows, Monitor, Inventory and Labs groups, Report a bug, Settings), the top bar with the Acme Lending workspace switcher and Search, and the Agents page listing six agents" />
    </Frame>

    If your sidebar shows only **Logs**, **Analytics**, **Alerts** and **Settings**, you are in a primary workspace: switch to a Ringg Space with the workspace switcher to build agents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open each Monitor item">
    Click through **Logs**, **Campaigns**, **Analytics**, **Evals** and **Alerts**. Logs has three tabs, **Phone**, **WhatsApp** and **Workflows**; Ananya's calls land under **Phone**. Campaigns is where the calling list goes in part 4.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open each Inventory item">
    **Knowledge base** holds the files and web pages agents look up. **Numbers** has a **Phone** tab, with **All Numbers** and **Number Pools**, and a **WhatsApp** tab. **Tools** holds tools you set up once and reuse in any agent; its URL is `/integrations`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the top bar">
    The first crumb is the workspace switcher; **Copy workspace ID** sits beside each workspace in its menu. **Search** opens the command palette (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>). The credits figure shows your balance (prepaid) or used / limit (postpaid); click it to open **Billing plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at Settings">
    **Settings**, at the bottom of the sidebar, has its own menu under **Workspace**, **Access** and **Billing**. Two items matter for these lessons: **Company details** (<Tooltip tip="Know your customer: the business verification Ringg needs before some calls.">KYC</Tooltip>), needed for some test calls, and **API key**, which the developer needs for the capstone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note where the IDs are">
    Each object has a copy button next to its ID:

    - Agent: beside the agent name in the top bar of the editor, or from the URL `/assistants/<agent_id>`.
    - Number: the **ID** column on **Numbers**.
    - Campaign: beside the ID in the campaign's detail panel.
    - Call: beside the call ID in the call panel in **Logs**.

    The API key is shown once, when generated under **Settings → API key**. Share it through a secret manager.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoint

<Check>
You can open every item under **Monitor** and **Inventory** from memory, the command palette opens with <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>, and you know which button or column holds each ID.
</Check>

## Exercise

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>) and type `Billing`. Expected result: the palette offers the **Billing plan** settings page; opening it shows your balance, concurrency, transactions and invoices.

## What you learned

- **Assistants** and **Workflows** sit at the top of the sidebar; the rest is under **Monitor**, **Inventory** and **Labs**, with **Settings** at the bottom.
- The command palette finds any section or settings page by name, and shortcuts open an agent, log or campaign by ID.
- Every ID a developer needs has a copy button on its own page, and the API key is shown only once.
