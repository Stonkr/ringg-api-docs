**Part 3: Making your agent smarter · Lesson 2 of 4 · 20 minutes**

## Objective

Create a knowledge base of Acme's EMI policies, attach it to Ananya and tell her when to use it, so she answers policy questions from the document instead of guessing.

## The scenario

Customers ask what happens if they pay late. Acme's policy: a grace period of 3 days after the due date, a late fee of ₹500 after that, and payment by UPI, net banking or at a branch. The prompt holds only what the agent needs on every call; policies go in a knowledge base, which other agents can share.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write the policy file">
    Create `emi-policy.csv` with two columns, `question` and `answer`, and one row per policy: the grace period, the late fee and the payment methods. Clear columns help the agent match answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the knowledge base">
    In the sidebar, open **Inventory → Knowledge base** and click **Create**. Set **Name** to `EMI policy`; it is saved as `emi_policy` and cannot be renamed later. On **Upload files**, drop the CSV. It opens in a preview with **Column Configuration** below; keep the **Type** Ringg guessed for each column and leave **Exact match** off. Click **Save**: "Knowledge base creation started".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Ready, then ask it a question">
    The file's chip goes from **Processing** to **Indexed**, and on the list the status goes from **Training** to **Ready**. Open the knowledge base; it opens on the file's contents, so click the back arrow to reach the test chat. Type "What is the late fee?" into **Ask a question about your data...**. The answer names the fee and lists the file it came from. If it is wrong here, fix the CSV before you touch the agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach it to Ananya">
    Open the agent, click **Knowledge Base** in the editor sidebar, then **Select Knowledge Base**, and tick `emi_policy`: "Knowledge base attached successfully". The button now shows the count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell her when to use it">
    In **Prompt**, in the **FAQs** section, type `@` and pick `emi_policy` from the **Knowledge Bases** group. Write: "For questions about late fees, the grace period or payment methods, answer from @emi_policy. If the answer is not there, say you will check and have an advisor call back." Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoint

<Check>
The **Knowledge Base** panel lists `emi_policy` below the button. Open **Test assistant**, pick **Live chat**, click **Start** and type "What happens if I pay late?": the reply gives the 3-day grace period and the ₹500 fee, the same answer the knowledge base's own chat gave.
</Check>

## Exercise

Click the trash icon on `emi_policy` in the **Knowledge Base** panel. Expected result: "Knowledge base detached successfully", and the `@emi_policy` mention in the prompt turns red, because the menu lists only attached knowledge bases. Attach it again and the chip is back to normal.

## What you learned

- Short facts the agent needs on every call go in the prompt; policies, price lists and FAQs go in a knowledge base, which several agents can share.
- A knowledge base must be **Ready** before an agent can use it. Test it in its own chat first, then in the agent.
- Attaching makes the knowledge base available, and the agent decides when to search it; the `@` mention tells her which questions it answers.
