**Part 3: Making your agent smarter · Lesson 4 of 4 · 15 minutes**

## Objective

Define the three facts Acme wants from every reminder call as typed Advanced Analysis variables, mark the goal, and test them on a call you already made.

## The scenario

After the call, Acme's collections team needs three things: did the customer promise to pay, by which date, and if not, why. Status, duration and transcript are already in Logs; these three are extracted from the transcript by Advanced Analysis after every conversation. `payment_promised` is the goal, so Analytics can report a success rate in part 6.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Advanced Analysis">
    In Ananya's editor, open **Advanced Settings → Advanced Analysis** and click **Add a new variable**. The **Add variables** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the goal">
    Set **Variable name** to `payment_promised` and keep **Type** on **Boolean**. For **Description**, write the instruction "True if the customer agreed to pay by a specific date." Set **Default value** to `false` and switch on **Use as goal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the other two">
    Click **Add new variable** inside the dialog for each:

    - `promised_date`, type **Date**, description "The date the customer promised to pay, if any."
    - `objection`, type **Enum**, with the options `already_paid`, `no_money`, `dispute` and `none` (type each and press Enter), description "The customer's main reason for not paying.", default value `none`.

    The button at the bottom now reads **Add 3**. Click it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add context and tool logs">
    Below the table, write in **Context** what counts as a promise: "A promise means the customer named a date, or said they would pay today." Click **Save**: "Context saved". Switch on **Log tool calls** so the analysis also sees what `crm_lookup` returned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test on your part 2 call">
    Click **Test**. In **Test advanced analysis**, pick the test call from part 2 under **Or select from recent call log**; picking it runs the test straight away. **Analysis result** appears next to **Call transcript**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoint

<Check>
The table lists the three variables with their **Type** and **Default value**, and `payment_promised` carries a **Goal** chip. In the test, **Analysis result** shows a value for each that matches what you said on the call: if you named a date, `payment_promised` is true and `promised_date` holds it.
</Check>

## Exercise

Add `amount_promised` as a **Number** with the description "The amount in rupees the customer said they would pay." and the default value `0`, then click **Test** on the same call. Expected result: a fourth row in the table and a fourth line in **Analysis result**, reading `0` if you named no amount on the call.

## What you learned

- Advanced Analysis variables are filled from the transcript after the call; custom variables are filled before it. They are separate lists.
- Only a Boolean can be the goal, and an agent has one at a time. Analytics' **Goal achieved** comes from it.
- Results appear in the call's **Advanced analysis** tab in Logs, in Logs filters, Analytics cohorts, post-call tools, alerts and the `client_analysis_completed` webhook, which the capstone uses to hand them to the CRM.
