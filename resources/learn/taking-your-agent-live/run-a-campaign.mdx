**Part 4: Taking your agent live · Lesson 2 of 4 · 20 minutes**

## Objective

Launch a campaign of three calls to numbers you own, and follow it from **Scheduled** to **Completed**.

## The scenario

Before Acme calls real customers, it runs the reminder over three internal numbers: yours and two colleagues who agree to answer. The flow is the same at thousands of rows. Make a CSV with the header row `callee_name,mobile_number,due_amount,due_date,loan_account` and three rows, phone numbers as ten digits without `+91`. Headers that match Ananya's variable names map themselves.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it and pick the agent">
    In the sidebar, open **Monitor → Campaigns** and click **Create campaign**. **Campaign Setup** opens on **Upload details**. Enter **Campaign name** `EMI reminders - dry run`, keep **Campaign type** on **Assistant**, and pick **EMI reminder - Hindi** under **Select assistant**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the list">
    Drag your CSV onto **Upload calling list** or click **Select File**. In **Calling list settings**, leave **Country code** on **+91** and check that every variable under **Map variables to CSV columns** found its column. Leave **Remove invalid rows** on. Click **Next**: "Processing CSV..." then "CSV verified successfully".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the calls">
    Under **Select number**, choose **Number pool** and pick **Acme EMI reminders**. Keep **Timezone** `Asia/Kolkata`; **Start date & time** defaults to 10 minutes from now and **End date & time** to a day later. **Calling windows** defaults to 08:00–20:00 every day; if you are outside those hours, click **Edit**, add a window that covers now, and **Save**. Set **Retry count** to **1 Retry** and **Cooldown Period** to **5 Minutes**. **Email notifications** already lists you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Click **Create campaign**. You see "Campaign created successfully!" and the campaign appears in the list as **Scheduled**. The three calls are real calls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it run">
    Once the start time passes inside the calling window, your phones ring. Click the campaign's row: the detail panel shows **Status**, **Assistant**, **Call time**, **Timezone** and **Retry configuration**. **History** opens the campaign's calls in Logs, with no date limit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoint

<Check>
When the attempts are done, the detail panel shows **Status** **Completed** with the buttons **Analytics**, **History** and **Export report**. **History** lists the three calls as **Completed**; a call you let ring first shows **Retry**, then **Completed** after the second attempt.
</Check>

## Exercise

Create a second campaign from the same file, then stop it before it starts: select its row and click **Terminate**. The dialog reads "Termination will stop all future calls. Are you sure you want to proceed?" Click **Confirm**. Expected result: "Campaign terminated", and because the campaign never placed a call it drops out of the list. There is no pause, and no resume from the dashboard.

## What you learned

- **Campaign Setup** has two steps: **Upload details** (name, agent, CSV, country code, variable mapping) and **Configure campaign** (numbers or pool, schedule, calling windows, retries, notifications).
- Calls go out only after the start time and inside a calling window, read in the campaign's timezone. Every retry is another call to the same person.
- The detail panel's **History** opens the campaign's calls in Logs; **Terminate** stops all future calls, and a terminated campaign can't be resumed from the dashboard.
