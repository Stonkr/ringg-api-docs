**Part 4: Taking your agent live · Lesson 4 of 4 · 15 minutes**

## Objective

Give Acme an agent that answers callbacks on its own number, and know where the web widget and WhatsApp agents live.

## The scenario

Customers see Acme's number on their phone and call it back; today nothing answers. An inbound agent answers calls to the one number attached to it, so Acme creates **Acme callbacks** and attaches a number. (An outbound agent with **Inbound Calling** enabled under **Advanced Settings** also answers calls, with a prompt per direction.)

A number attached to an agent can't be in a pool, and Acme's only number is in the **Acme EMI reminders** pool. Buy a second one as in lesson 1, or remove this one from the pool with its row's remove button under **Number Pools**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the inbound agent">
    Click **Assistants**, then **Create agent**, and choose the **Inbound** tab. Pick a template, set **Agent Name** to `Acme callbacks`, the languages and a voice as in part 2, and click **Next**. Fill in the call context, leave **Select Phone Number** empty, and click **Create Assistant**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the number">
    Expand **Attach Number** under **Call Settings** in the editor sidebar. Open **Inbound Number** and choose the number: "Number has been attached successfully", with no separate save. The list shows only numbers not attached to another agent that have inbound calling enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call it">
    Dial the number from your phone. The agent answers, and the call appears at the top of **Logs**. A number on your own provider account also needs provider-side routing to Ringg, such as the `Ringg Inbound` applet in Exotel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Embed & Widgets">
    Open **EMI reminder - Hindi**, then **Advanced Settings → Embed & Widgets**. Every agent except a WhatsApp agent has it. **Install & domains** holds the **Web** and **Flutter** snippets and **Allowed clients**, the sites and apps that may run the agent; **Appearance** sets the widget's look; **Components** builds the cards and forms it can show in a chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See where a WhatsApp agent starts">
    A WhatsApp agent has the same prompt, knowledge base, variables and tools, but replies on a WhatsApp Business number connected through Meta: [connect WhatsApp Business](/documentation/inventory/tools-integrations/connect-whatsapp), then **Create agent → WhatsApp**. Its chats land in **Logs → WhatsApp**; Analytics does not cover them. The [WhatsApp overview](/documentation/build-agents/agents/whatsapp-agents/overview) has the setup order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoint

<Check>
On **Numbers → All Numbers**, the number's **Used By** column shows **Acme callbacks** with a **Detach Number** button, and your call sits at the top of Logs.
</Check>

## What you learned

- An inbound agent answers calls to the one number attached to it under **Attach Number**; an outbound agent's caller ID is picked per call or campaign instead.
- One agent has one inbound number and one number serves one agent. A number attached to an agent can't join a pool.
- **Embed & Widgets** is on every agent except WhatsApp agents; a WhatsApp agent needs a connected WhatsApp Business number and reports to **Logs → WhatsApp**.
