**Part 4: Taking your agent live · Lesson 1 of 4 · 15 minutes**

## Objective

Have a number of your own in the workspace, inside a number pool, ready to be the caller ID for Acme's first campaign.

## The scenario

So far Ananya has called from Ringg's shared test numbers. They are for testing only: the campaign form does not offer them, and they can't join a pool. Acme needs its own number. And because one line that dials hundreds of customers soon gets flagged as spam, the number goes into a pool, which rotates across its numbers and skips flagged ones.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add Numbers">
    In the sidebar, open **Inventory → Numbers**. On the **Phone** tab, under **All Numbers**, click **Add number**. The **Add Numbers** dialog lists providers on the left, with Plivo selected. Your workspace role needs the **Numbers** permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the provider and account">
    Keep **Plivo** and choose **Ringg** in the telephony account selector: Ringg holds the line and bills a rental rate per billing period. If Acme already had numbers with a provider such as Exotel, you would select that provider, click the gear (**Configure provider**) to connect the account, and use the **Import your own** tab instead; the steps are on the [buy or import](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/buy-or-import-a-number#import-a-number-you-already-own) page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where the number is from">
    Pick **India** and a region, or leave the region on **All**. If the **Buy new** tab says "No new numbers available.", widen the region.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy it">
    On **Buy new**, each row shows the number and its **Rental Rate**, for example `₹499 / 1 Month`. Click **Buy Number** on one and confirm with **Buy Number**. You see "Purchase has been completed successfully." and the number appears in **All Numbers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the pool">
    Open **Number Pools** and click **Add pool**. Set **Pool name** to `Acme EMI reminders`, open **Add numbers to pool** and tick your new number. Switch on **Auto purchase** so the pool buys a replacement if the number is flagged as spam. The replacement comes from the flagged number's own country; set **Replacement country** to India as the fallback. Click **Create pool**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoint

<Check>
You see "Pool created" and a card for **Acme EMI reminders** showing **1 Numbers**. Back on **All Numbers**, the number's **Pool** column names the pool.
</Check>

## Exercise

Tag the number so your team can tell it apart from the ones Acme adds later. On its row in **All Numbers**, click **Add Tags**, type `collections` in **Enter tag** and click **Add**. Expected result: the tag shows in the **Tags** column and in number pickers from now on. Test numbers can't be tagged, which is one more way to spot them.

## What you learned

- Every number enters the workspace through **Add Numbers**: bought on the **Ringg** account or on a provider account you connected, imported from that account, or typed in by hand.
- Test numbers are for test calls only; they are not offered to campaigns and can't join a pool.
- A pool rotates caller IDs, skips spam-flagged numbers and, with **Auto purchase**, buys replacements. A number belongs to at most one pool.
