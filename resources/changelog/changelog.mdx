<Update label="19 October 2026" description="Live data in the Flow builder" tags={["WhatsApp"]}>
  Build a WhatsApp Flow that loads live data without Flow JSON. In the [Flow builder](/documentation/build-agents/agents/whatsapp-agents/flow-builder#load-live-data), a page's button can load the next page from the data source, and choices and date pickers can take their options and dates from it. Start from the **Appointment booking** or **Lookup from your server** template. For [your own server](/documentation/build-agents/agents/whatsapp-agents/flows-your-server), the Flow's **Data source** shows each request Ringg sends and the reply to return.
</Update>

<Update label="18 October 2026" description="WhatsApp Flows" tags={["WhatsApp"]}>
  New [WhatsApp Flows](/documentation/build-agents/agents/whatsapp-agents/flows): a WhatsApp agent sends a form inside the chat, like a booking, a lead form or a survey, and gets the answers back as the next message. A message template can open a Flow from a button too. Build Flows in the [Flow builder](/documentation/build-agents/agents/whatsapp-agents/flow-builder) or sync them from WhatsApp Manager, and fill their screens with [live data](/documentation/build-agents/agents/whatsapp-agents/flows-live-data) from your calendar or [your own server](/documentation/build-agents/agents/whatsapp-agents/flows-your-server).
</Update>

<Update label="16 October 2026" description="Analytics API" tags={["API"]}>
  The [analytics API](/api-reference/monitoring/analytics/get-volume-analytics) now returns the same figures as the [Analytics](/documentation/monitor/analytics) page, with filters for agent version, channel, callbacks and Ringg Spaces. It covers volume, connectivity, agent performance, latency, concurrency, cohorts and chat, across 11 endpoints under `/analytics/v4`. The five earlier analytics endpoint pages are replaced, and their URLs redirect to the new pages.
</Update>

<Update label="15 October 2026" description="Members permission" tags={["Account"]}>
  New **Members** permission: give it to a member, for example your IT admin, and they can invite members, change their permissions and remove them without being the owner. They can only grant or remove the areas they hold themselves, and can't change the owner. Granted in a primary workspace, it also applies in the [Ringg Spaces](/documentation/manage-account/ringg-spaces/overview) the member inherits; a Ringg Space they were invited to directly keeps its own permissions. See [Let a member manage members](/documentation/manage-account/members#let-a-member-manage-members).
</Update>

<Update label="13 October 2026" description="Docs" tags={["Docs"]}>
  New guides to connect [Telnyx](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/your-own-telephony/telnyx) and [InTalk](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/your-own-telephony/intalk) as your own telephony. Both are import only, and are now listed in [supported providers](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/overview#supported-telephony-providers), the [account fields](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/buy-or-import-a-number#connect-a-provider-account-first) and [provider-side routing](/documentation/inventory/phone-numbers/inbound-agent-number#provider-side-routing).

  API reference URLs now follow the sidebar groups, for example [`/api-reference/calling/individual-calls/initiate-individual-call`](/api-reference/calling/individual-calls/initiate-individual-call). Old `/api-reference/endpoint/…` links redirect to the new pages.
</Update>

<Update label="29 September 2026" description="API keys" tags={["Account"]}>
  New workspace [API keys](/documentation/manage-account/api-key) start with `rg_live_` and show a masked form in the dashboard so you can tell keys apart. Keys generated earlier (`ringg_live_` or a plain UUID) keep working until you regenerate them.
</Update>

<Update label="28 September 2026" description="WhatsApp agents" tags={["WhatsApp", "API"]}>
  - A WhatsApp chat now ends by the agent's idle timeout or maximum length, which you set per agent under session timeouts; see [Create a WhatsApp agent](/documentation/build-agents/agents/whatsapp-agents/create-an-agent).
  - New **End the chat** on-call tool for chat (End Call stays voice-only), and agents can reply to earlier messages in the thread.
  - New API: [start a WhatsApp conversation](/documentation/deploy/whatsapp/start-a-conversation) from your backend.
</Update>

<Update label="24 September 2026" description="WhatsApp" tags={["WhatsApp"]}>
  WhatsApp agents documented end to end: [overview](/documentation/build-agents/agents/whatsapp-agents/overview), Meta Embedded Signup [onboarding](/documentation/inventory/tools-integrations/connect-whatsapp) with numbers and templates, [on-call](/documentation/build-agents/customize-agent-behaviour/tools/whatsapp-tools#on-call) and [post-call](/documentation/build-agents/customize-agent-behaviour/tools/whatsapp-tools#post-call) tools, [creating an agent](/documentation/build-agents/agents/whatsapp-agents/create-an-agent) and attaching a number, [chat events](/documentation/build-agents/customize-agent-behaviour/webhooks/whatsapp-chat-events#whatsapp-chat-events), and [chat history](/documentation/monitor/logs#whatsapp-chats) with CSV export.
