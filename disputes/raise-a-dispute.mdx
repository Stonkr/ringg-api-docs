If you think the AI scored one of your calls wrongly, appeal it: pick the parameters it got wrong and say why for each. A QA re-scores the call, and their score becomes final.

In [Ringg Analytics](https://analytics.ringg.ai): **Sidebar → Interactions →** open your call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the call">
    On **Interactions**, filter **Appeal** to **Can still appeal** to list the calls you can still dispute, and open one. Under "Think the AI got this wrong?" the card shows the deadline: "Appeal by …".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Appeal this score">
    The form lists every parameter with the AI's rating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the parameters and explain each">
    Tick each parameter you dispute. Its box opens with the AI's reason; write why it is wrong, at least 20 characters. Point the QA to the moment in the call, for example "at 0:20".

    <Frame caption="Two parameters disputed, each with a reason">
      <img src="/media/disputes-raise/appeal-form.webp" alt="Appeal form with Gave the recording disclosure and Stated the EMI amount and due date ticked, each showing the AI's rating No, the AI's reason and the agent's explanation, and the other parameters unticked with their AI ratings" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note if needed, then submit">
    Add anything else for the QA in the optional note. Click **Review & submit**, then **Submit appeal**. You can appeal a call only once, and can't edit the appeal after you submit it.

    <Check>The call shows "Waiting for a QA to pick it up." You and your team lead get an email: "Appeal submitted: …".</Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Follow your appeals

Filter **Appeal** on **Interactions**: **Appealed — awaiting QA** for open ones, **Appeal resolved** for the rest. The call's status changes to "A QA is re-scoring your call." when a QA picks it up. See [Results and corrections](/disputes/results-and-corrections) for what you get back.

## When you can't appeal

**Appeal this score** isn't offered when disputes are off, or the call isn't yours, isn't a call, isn't scored yet or already has an appeal. Otherwise the card says why:

| Message | Why |
|---|---|
| "The appeal window for this call has closed" | The window, counted from when the AI scored the call, has passed |
| "This call is being re-scored — you can appeal once it finishes" | The call is being reprocessed |
