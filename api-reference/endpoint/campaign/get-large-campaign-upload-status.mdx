Poll it every few seconds after [Upload large campaign CSV](/api-reference/endpoint/campaign/upload-large-campaign-csv), until `status` stops changing.

## Statuses

| `status` | Meaning | Next |
|---|---|---|
| `verifying` | Rows are still being validated | Keep polling |
| `draft` | The list is valid and ready | [Start calls](/api-reference/endpoint/campaign/make-large-campaign-calls-async) |
| `no_valid_contacts` | Every row was invalid | Fix the CSV and upload it again |
| `insufficient_balance` | The workspace balance does not cover the list | Add balance, then [re-check the balance](/api-reference/endpoint/campaign/check-large-campaign-balance) |
| `failed` | Validation could not finish | Upload again |

The response also reports `total_callees`, the valid contacts loaded, and `invalidated_rows_count`, the rows that failed validation. After launch, `status` follows the campaign: `preparing`, `in_db`, `scheduled`, `ongoing`, `completed` or `terminated`.
