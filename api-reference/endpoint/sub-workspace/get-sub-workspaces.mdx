Use it for a tenant dashboard in your own admin tool, to check headroom before allocating concurrency or credit, and to reconcile per-client usage at month end.

## Reading the response

- `family.concurrency_available` is what [Allocate Concurrency](/api-reference/endpoint/sub-workspace/allocate-concurrency) can still hand out; `family.available_pot` (prepaid) is what [Reallocate Credit Balances](/api-reference/endpoint/sub-workspace/reallocate-credit-balances) can still move out of the primary.
- `family.is_individual_entity` tells you the billing mode: `true` for individual (each Ringg Space has its own wallet or limit), `false` for common (one pool on the primary). The keys present in `family` and in each row's `billing` depend on it; the response schema below lists them per mode.
- `usage_this_period` is the value of completed calls since the 1st of the current month in the `Asia/Kolkata` timezone, in the workspace's billing currency.
- Amounts are JSON numbers here (`200000.0`); the two allocation endpoints return decimal strings.

## Notes

- Works on a primary with no Ringg Spaces yet; `sub_workspaces` is then an empty list.
- Archived Ringg Spaces are not listed.

## Errors

- `400`: `Sub-workspaces cannot have their own sub-workspaces.` — the key belongs to a Ringg Space that has Ringg Spaces enabled.
- `401`: Invalid or missing API key.
- `403`: `Sub-workspaces are not enabled for this workspace.` — Ringg Spaces are not enabled on the primary, or the key belongs to a Ringg Space.
