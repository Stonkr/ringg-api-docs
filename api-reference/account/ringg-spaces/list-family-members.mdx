Members of the primary (`scope: "primary"`) inherit access to every current and future Ringg Space; people added directly to a Ringg Space (`scope: "sub"`) see only the Ringg Spaces in their `sub_workspace_ids`. Use it for access audits across client or team workspaces, or to show who can reach a Ringg Space in your own admin tool.

## Notes

- `permissions_by_workspace` already includes inherited rows, so render access from it rather than deriving it from `scope`.
- The endpoint only reads. Add or remove people in the dashboard ([Workspace members](/documentation/manage-account/members)).

## Errors

- `400`: `Sub-workspaces cannot have their own sub-workspaces.` — the key belongs to a Ringg Space that has Ringg Spaces enabled.
- `401`: Invalid or missing API key.
- `403`: `Sub-workspaces are not enabled for this workspace.` — Ringg Spaces are not enabled on the primary, or the key belongs to a Ringg Space.
