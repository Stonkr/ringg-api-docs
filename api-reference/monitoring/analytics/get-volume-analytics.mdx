Returns the numbers behind the **Volume** cards and trend chart in [Analytics](/documentation/monitor/analytics). Each card has `count`, `previous` (the period of the same length just before) and `delta_pct`.

## Notes

- `sum(by_bucket[].total)` equals `total.count`, and in each bucket `total = completed + failed + cancelled + registered + ongoing + retry`.
- `total.campaign_count` and `total.api_count` split the current total by where calls came from. A callback counts toward its parent call's campaign.
- `callback_count` always reports callback calls, whatever `include_callback` is set to. It overlaps `total` only when `include_callback=true`.
- `connection_rate.campaign_time_exhausted` and `connection_rate.retry_exhausted` count calls stopped by the campaign's end time or retry limit.
- `aht_seconds.duration_distribution` counts completed calls in four buckets: under 10 seconds, 10 to 30, 30 to 60, and 60 or more.
- `unique_numbers_used` is the phone numbers your workspace owns, so it ignores the date range and filters.
- With a call `channel` filter, chats are left out and `chat_total.count` is `0`.

## Errors

- `400`: a filter is invalid, such as an unknown `channel`, a malformed ID, or `sub_workspace_id` sent from a workspace that isn't a primary workspace.
- `401`: `X-API-KEY` is missing or invalid.
