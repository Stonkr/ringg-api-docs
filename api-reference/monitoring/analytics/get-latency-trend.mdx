Plot `bucket` on the X-axis and `p50_ms` and `p95_ms` on the Y-axis. Compare before and after a prompt, model or voice change to catch regressions.

## Notes

- `scored` is the number of calls in the bucket with latency data. Buckets with few scored calls are noisy.
- Bucket size follows the date range: hour, day, week (labelled with the Monday) or month (labelled with the 1st). Pass `tz` to bucket in your timezone.

## Errors

- `400`: a filter is invalid, such as an unknown `channel`, a malformed ID, or `sub_workspace_id` sent from a workspace that isn't a primary workspace.
- `401`: `X-API-KEY` is missing or invalid.
