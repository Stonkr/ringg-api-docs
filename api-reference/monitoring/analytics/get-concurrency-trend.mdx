Returns the **Concurrency usage** chart in [Analytics](/documentation/monitor/analytics). If `received` keeps reaching `paid`, your calls are waiting on concurrency: raise it on [Billing](/documentation/manage-account/billing#change-concurrency).

## Notes

- It covers the whole workspace and takes only the date range, `tz` and `sub_workspace_id`.
- `granularity` is `hour` for ranges of two days or less and `day` otherwise.
- `available` is `false` when there is no data for the range.

## Errors

- `400`: a filter is invalid, such as an unknown `channel`, a malformed ID, or `sub_workspace_id` sent from a workspace that isn't a primary workspace.
- `401`: `X-API-KEY` is missing or invalid.
