Returns the data behind the **Connectivity** chart in [Analytics](/documentation/monitor/analytics). Use `by_retry_attempt` to judge whether more retries are worth it: `attempt_2` and later are calls that connected only after a retry.

## Notes

- `connectivity_pct` runs from `0` to `100`, rounded to two decimals, unlike the `0` to `1` rates on other analytics endpoints. It is `0.0` when there are no calls.
- `total` includes every call status, including registered, ongoing and retry calls.
- For calls stopped by the campaign's end time or retry limit, read `volume.connection_rate` from [Get volume analytics](/api-reference/monitoring/analytics/get-volume-analytics).

## Errors

- `400`: a filter is invalid, such as an unknown `channel`, a malformed ID, or `sub_workspace_id` sent from a workspace that isn't a primary workspace.
- `401`: `X-API-KEY` is missing or invalid.
