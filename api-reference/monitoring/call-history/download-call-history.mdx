The response only confirms the export was queued and lists who will get the link: `{"message": "Call history export has been queued. You will receive an email shortly.", "emails": ["owner@example.com"]}`. The download link arrives by email; large exports take several minutes.

## Choose who receives the export

By default the link goes to the requester; with an API key, that is the workspace owner. To send it to specific teammates instead, add `email`. Repeat it for several people.

<Warning>
**Each recipient must be a member of your workspace.** The export is only emailed to people who belong to the workspace: they were invited, accepted the invite, and haven't been removed. An address outside the workspace gets a `400` and no one receives the export. To send it to someone new, invite them to the workspace first.
</Warning>

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url 'https://prod-api.ringg.ai/ca/api/v0/calling/history/download?start_date=2025-09-25T00:00:00%2B05:30&end_date=2025-09-25T23:59:59%2B05:30&email=ops@example.com&email=qa@example.com' \
  --header 'X-API-KEY: <x-api-key>'
```

- A comma-separated list (`email=ops@example.com,qa@example.com`) works too.
- The link goes only to these addresses, one email each. The workspace owner is not copied.
- Every address must belong to an active member of your workspace: invite accepted and not removed. Otherwise the request returns `400` naming the address, and no one is emailed. Invite them to the workspace first.

## Notes

- `call_type` is `inbound`, `outbound` or `webcall`. The export covers every call that matches the filters.
- `include_analysis=true` adds the platform and [Advanced Analysis](/documentation/build-agents/customize-agent-behaviour/advanced-analysis) results.
- The CSV holds call details, status and timestamps, agent and campaign, transcript and recording links, and custom variables.
- The emailed link is valid for 7 days and downloads a ZIP with one CSV.
- Recording links in the export may expire; fetch a fresh link from [Get call details](/api-reference/monitoring/call-history/get-call-details).
- `409` means the same export is already running; `429` means too many exports are running, so try again later.
