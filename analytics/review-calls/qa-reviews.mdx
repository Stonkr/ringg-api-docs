A QA review is a QA's own scoring of a call, done whenever they choose. It sits next to the AI's score and doesn't replace it; to change the official score of a call, the agent [disputes](/disputes/overview) it and a QA resolves the dispute.

In [Ringg Analytics](https://analytics.ringg.ai): **Sidebar → Interactions →** open a call. Owners, admins and QAs can review.

## Review a call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the call">
    Open it from **Interactions**, or **Open full page** for more room.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rate the parameters you check">
    Expand a category and pick a rating for each parameter you check, with a comment saying why. Parameters you don't rate keep the AI's rating in your **Human** score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the call fatal if it is">
    Tick **Mark interaction as fatal** to set the Human score to 0, whatever the ratings, and say why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save review**, or **Discard** to drop your changes.

    <Check>The scorecard shows **AI** and **Human** scores side by side.</Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

**View detailed comparison** lists the parameters where you and the AI disagree, or says "Fully aligned". Every save is kept in the call's review history, marked **Added**, **Edited** or **Cleared**, with who made it.

## Rules

- A QA set to **Rate and comment on every parameter** can't save until each parameter has a rating and a comment: "Rate and comment on every parameter to save: N left." Clearing a whole review is always allowed.
- A QA in **Blind review** sees AI ratings, reasons and summaries as `***` while they score, so their ratings stay independent. Admins set both on **Team** (see [QA settings](/analytics/roles-and-access#qa-settings)).
- Use **Human Evaluation** in the **Interactions** filter to list reviewed or unreviewed calls.

## Share reviews with agents

To email the agent each time a QA saves a review, add a notification trigger that starts when **A QA reviews a call**. The ready-made **QA Review Shared** template lists the QA's score against the AI's and every rating and comment. See [Notifications](/analytics/set-up/notifications).
